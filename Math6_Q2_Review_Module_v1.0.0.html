Step-by-Step Solver 🧮 ⏱ ~12 min

Four walkthroughs: GEMDAS first step, keep-change-flip fractions, polynomials, and volume.

➕ Solver A - GEMDAS First Step

Grouping innermost first. Exponents. MD multiply and divide left to right. AS add and subtract left to right. Do not do all multiplication before all division.

➗ Solver B - Keep-Change-Flip Fractions

Keep the first fraction, change ÷ to ×, flip the second (multiply by the reciprocal). Whole 8 has reciprocal 1/8, not 8. Type 1/2 or mixed 1 1/2.

🔣 Solver C - Polynomial Classifier

Count terms for monomial, binomial, trinomial. Not a polynomial if a variable is in a denominator, as an exponent, with a negative exponent, or inside a radical. √225 is the constant 15 — a monomial.

📦 Solver D - Volume (Prism vs Pyramid)

Rectangular prism V = lwh (Yellow Material). Triangular prism V = bhH / 2. Pyramid V = lwh / 3 (Hollow Material square pyramid s²h / 3). π = 3.14. Trap: triangular pyramid /6, not /2.
