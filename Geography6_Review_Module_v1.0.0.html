Step-by-Step Solver 🧮 ⏱ ~12 min

Four interactive walkthroughs for the topics that mix people up the most: variables, Earth layers, plate boundaries, and quake vocab (magnitude vs intensity, focus vs epicenter). Tap, try, and walk through it. No shame in hints.

🔬 Solver A - Variable Sorter

Independent = what you change on purpose. Dependent = what you measure. Controlled = what you keep the same so the test is fair.

🌍 Solver B - Layer Matcher

Chemical layers = what it is made of (crust, mantle, core). Physical layers = how it behaves (lithosphere, asthenosphere, mesosphere, outer core, inner core). Do not mix those two lists.

Tap a sample, then pick the layer that matches the clue.

🧩 Solver C - Boundary Classifier

Convergent = plates collide. Divergent = plates move apart (new crust). Transform = plates slide past (earthquakes). Tap the picture that matches the clue.

📳 Solver D - Quake Vocab

Focus (hypocenter) is where the quake starts inside the Earth. Epicenter is the point on the surface above the focus. Magnitude = energy released. Intensity = shaking or damage felt in a place. This pair is easy to mix up.

Tap the diagram: which dot is the focus? Then pick magnitude or intensity for the clue.
