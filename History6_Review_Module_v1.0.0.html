⛵ Solver B - Barangay Matcher

🗺️ Solver D - Map Vocab

Benham Rise is east of Luzon. West Philippine Sea is the west EEZ. Philippine Trench / Galathea Depth is the deep. Do not stack those three into one blob.

Tap west, east, or the deep trench. Then pick the name that matches the clue.
