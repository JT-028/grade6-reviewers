Step-by-Step Solver 🧮 ⏱ ~12 min

Four walkthroughs: Japanese colonial occupation, the six constitutions, president programs, and EDSA.

🇯🇵 Solver A - Japanese Colonial Occupation

Japan occupied the Philippines from 1942 to 1945. Lock the occupation tool (KALIBAPI, Laurel's puppet republic, Kempeitai, HUKBALAHAP, Mickey Mouse Money), not how islands formed.

📜 Solver B - Constitution Matcher

Six constitutions since 1898: 1899 Malolos, 1935, 1943 Laurel, 1935 restored, 1973, 1987. Aquino also issued a 1986 Freedom Constitution.

Tap a sample, then pick the constitution.

🏛️ Solver C - President Programs

Match the program, not the nickname. Magsaysay ≠ Lubao. Green Revolution = Marcos Sr. K-12 = Aquino III. BBB = Duterte. The three cards are the ones students mix most.

✊ Solver D - EDSA Timeline

Four days: February 22–25, 1986. Enrile and Ramos at Camp Crame. Cardinal Sin on Radio Veritas. Marcos flees to Hawaii. Peaceful People Power.

Tap the diagram: red camp (defectors) or gold EDSA crowd. Then name the beat.
