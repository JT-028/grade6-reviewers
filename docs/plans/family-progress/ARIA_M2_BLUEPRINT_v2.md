Download failure/open-preview failure has an honest retry/manual-save path; preparation failure enables no partial apparently complete export.

Technical mechanics verified against the primary [Web Share specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/web-share/) (transient activation, capability and user-agent targets) and [HTML canvas serialization specification](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/canvas.html#dom-canvas-toblob-dev) (asynchronous toBlob, nullable result and format fallback), checked 2026-10-24. Runtime capability and real-device validation remain mandatory; no browser/OS/Messenger delivery guarantee follows from these specifications.
