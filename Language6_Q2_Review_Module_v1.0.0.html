Step-by-Step Solver 🧩 ⏱ ~12 min

Four walkthroughs: direct vs indirect objects, pronoun cases, verbals, and propaganda techniques.

📝 Solver A - Direct, Indirect, Object of the Preposition

DO answers What? or Whom? and receives the action. IO answers To/For whom? and sits between the verb and the DO. OP follows a preposition.

👤 Solver B - Pronoun Cases

Nominative = subject or predicate nominative (I, we, he, she, they, who). Objective = DO, IO, OP (me, us, him, her, them, whom). Possessive = ownership (mine, ours, hers, theirs).

Tap a sample, then pick the case.

⚡ Solver C - Verbal Classifier

Gerund = -ing used as a noun. Participle = adjective (present -ing or past). Infinitive = to + base. Falling leaves is a participle trap, not a gerund.

📢 Solver D - Propaganda Matcher

Seven techniques. These four mix people up most: bandwagon, testimonial, card stacking, transfer. “Quote a barangay captain” is a distractor, not card stacking.
