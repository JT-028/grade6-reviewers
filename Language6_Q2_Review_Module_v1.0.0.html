Step-by-Step Solver 🧩 ⏱ ~12 min

Four walkthroughs: direct vs indirect objects, pronoun cases, verbals, and propaganda techniques.

📝 Solver A - Nominative Functions

S subject (does the action). PN predicate nominative (renames the subject after is/are). AP appositive (extra name, commas, can lift out). DA direct address (talking TO someone, commas). Do not mix AP and DA.

👤 Solver B - Pronoun Kinds

Reflexive is essential (sentence breaks without it). Intensive just emphasizes (you can lift it out). Possessive shows ownership. Relative starts a clause (who/whom/which/that). Compound relative adds -ever / -soever.

Tap a sample, then pick the kind.

⚡ Solver C - Verbals

Io suffered; Europa mostly did not. Odysseus said his name was Nobody and escaped under sheep, not cows. Zeus approached Europa as a bull, not a horse. Hyacinthus died by a discus, not a javelin.

💬 Solver D - Idioms, Analogies, Citations

Idioms keep their whole meaning. Analogies keep a relationship. In-text citations need last name + year, not the full title. Formal predicate nominative uses I, not me.
